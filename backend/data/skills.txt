# languages, frameworks and hard skills a resume reading or Tailor may add
# to a bank. One skill a line, its other spellings after a "|". Matching
# ignores case, spaces versus hyphens, a ".js" ending and a final plural
# "s", so "ReactJS", "React.js" and "react" are one line. No soft skills,
# degrees or vague phrases ("AI tools"): those are what the catalog keeps out.
# Written for WorkIt; add a line when a real skill is missing.
