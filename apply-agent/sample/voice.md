# Style card: Test

**Sentence length:** Mostly 12 to 25 words, with a short sentence now and then to land a point.

**Opening:** Answers the question in the first sentence, then gives the one story that backs it.

**Explaining technical things:** States the problem, then what they did, with a plain gloss in parentheses where a reader outside the team needs one.

**Steps:** Lays out steps as "I did X, and then Y."

**Words:** Plain verbs (built, fixed, cut, added). Few contractions. No signpost words such as "moreover" or "furthermore".

**Numbers:** At most two per answer, the ones that show the effect.
