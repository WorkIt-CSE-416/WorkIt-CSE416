Workday Sign-Up

Each company's Workday asks you to make an account before you apply. Set one email and password here, and WorkIt uses them to make your account and sign you in at every company. Without one, WorkIt makes a new password for each company.

  • At least 12 characters
  • An uppercase letter
  • A lowercase letter
  • A number
  • A special character

Your password stays in this browser. WorkIt never sends or saves it anywhere else.

Sites You Allowed

Employer career sites where you allowed WorkIt from its toolbar button. WorkIt reads only these sites, besides the job systems it fills. Remove one to take WorkIt off it.

    Fields to Autofill

    Turn off a kind of field and WorkIt leaves it blank for you to answer, and lists it under Needs Your Answer. Turning one off never deletes what you saved.

    WorkIt Tab

    The WorkIt tab sits on the right edge of every job application WorkIt can fill. Alt+W and WorkIt's toolbar button open the panel even where the tab is hidden.
