Workday Sign-Up

Each company's Workday asks you to make an account before you apply. Set one email and password here, and WorkIt uses them to make your account and sign you in at every company. WorkIt makes no Workday account until you set them.

  • At least 12 characters
  • An uppercase letter
  • A lowercase letter
  • A number
  • A special character

A new password applies to the companies WorkIt makes an account at after you change it: each company's Workday keeps its own account. Your password stays in this browser. WorkIt never sends or saves it anywhere else.

Saved Company Logins

The Workday login WorkIt keeps for each company, the one it signs you in with there. Removing the extension deletes them, so export them first if you need them.

    Sites You Allowed

    Employer career sites where you allowed WorkIt from its toolbar button. WorkIt reads only these sites, besides the job systems it fills. Remove one to take WorkIt off it.
